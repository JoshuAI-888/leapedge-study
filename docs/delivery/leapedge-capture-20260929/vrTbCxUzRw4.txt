SUMMARY

The creator outlines a strategic accumulation framework for precious metals miners that prioritizes buying technically strong leaders in confirmed uptrends rather than cheap, lagging names. Strong stocks trading above upward-sloping 50-day and 200-day moving averages should be bought on orderly pullbacks, while technically weak stocks should be avoided until deep market corrections offer steep discounts.

KEY POINTS

01

Adopt a strategic, incremental approach to buying mining stocks rather than a binary buy-or-sell mentality.

02

Prioritize buying technically strong mining stocks that are already in clear uptrends with 50-day and 200-day moving averages pointed upward.

03

Avoid chasing stocks like Banyan Gold that are extended; wait for shallow pullbacks even if they are fundamentally sound.

04

Utilize the 'strength comes to strength' principle, as institutional investors tend to favor stocks with established positive momentum.

05

New Pacific Metals is highlighted as an attractive swing trade because it recently bounced off its 50-day moving average.

06

Avoid buying stocks that are currently below their 200-day and 50-day moving averages, such as McEwen Mining, as 'weakness comes to weakness'.

07

Nexgold Mining is identified as a strong performer that formed a bull flag and maintained strength during recent gold corrections.

08

Delay purchases of weaker stocks until a market correction occurs, as they typically drop harder than strong stocks during downturns.

09

Ignore fundamentals for gold stocks, as gold is a global commodity with high liquidity regardless of the production location.

10

Perfect market entries are rare and often lucky; aim for an attractive average entry price across your entire mining portfolio.

## Cohort audit and remaining limitations

The twenty-case retained sources were inspected for extracted company names, claims and mentions. This expansion addresses omitted AI infrastructure issuers, foundation-model publishers, consumer and financial firms, the long podcast, and mining names. Ticker/name mapping remains distinct: garbled names such as “Cororeweave”, “Transdime”, “new”, “enter”, “Sur” and “GGDs” are not automatically resolved by this registry. Broad sector/commodity/index references do not identify an issuer.

The old `alphabet.com` entry was removed: its [own page](https://www.alphabet.com/en-ww.html) describes BMW-backed fleet leasing, not Alphabet Inc. [abc.xyz](https://abc.xyz/investor/) identifies Alphabet investor relations. Nu's old [investors.nu](https://www.investors.nu) address redirects to [nu.com/en/investors](https://nu.com/en/investors), which is now covered. Meta's IR page directly links the approved corporate/about and newsroom hosts; Facebook user content is not approved. Reddit approval is corporate `redditinc.com`, never `reddit.com` user posts. OpenAI community subdomains are not approved.

Not yet verified in this pass: RH, Celsius, Wynn, Hermès, AppLovin, NextGold, Cloudflare, AeroVironment, Casey's, Dave and Circle (attempted official pages were unavailable to the browser tool). Other names still needing ownership review include WhiteFiber, SK Hynix, Samsung, SanDisk, GlobalFoundries, Aeluma, Strategy, SpaceX, Devon Energy, Diamondback and ETF/index sponsors. Their SEC filings can still be returned through the verified SEC host; unknown issuer websites remain unknown. Do not interpret missing primary search results as absence of evidence or a negative company fact.

Pipeline and speculative prefetch use the same registry through `researchStep`. The exact registered-domain list remains part of the retrieval/cache identity, so newly expanded ownership scope cannot silently reuse a narrower historical search. Publisher classification additionally uses exact approved hosts; spoofed suffixes, invented subdomains and unknown domains remain unknown. Date confirmation, video cutoff/current separation, exact assertion support and independent audit requirements are unchanged.
